Purdue UniversitySpring 2026

ME 697

Advanced Scientific Machine Learning

An advanced course exploring state-of-the-art methods in scientific machine learning, including neural operators, infinite-dimensional Bayesian inverse problems, sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy), and calibration of stochastic differential equations. Students learn functional programming in JAX and use differentiable and probabilistic programming to implement these methods.

Online textbook by Ilias Bilionis ↗
Purdue UniversityFall 2024 · Fall 2025

ME 539

Introduction to Scientific Machine Learning

An introduction to data science and machine learning requiring no prior experience in either field. Topics include probability, Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation, supervised and unsupervised learning, state space models, physics-informed deep learning, and Bayesian inference. A focus on first principles prepares students to build and fit their own models.

Online textbook by Ilias Bilionis ↗
