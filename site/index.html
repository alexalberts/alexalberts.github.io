About me

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor at Northwestern University in the Department of Engineering Sciences and Applied Mathematics. I develop mathematical frameworks and computational methods for recovering quantities that cannot be measured directly using known physical laws or other prior information. I am interested in how probabilistic physical models can be defined, what their assumptions mean, and how they behave when combined with experimental data.

I received my PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Purdue University in 2024, advised by Ilias Bilionis, and continued at Purdue as a postdoctoral researcher until 2026. Before that, I earned my BS and MS in Applied Mathematics at the University of Akron.
